# Communic8 — "Current Stage Assessment"
**Website interactive section — full build spec**
**For:** AI Engineer (build) | **Owner:** Ajinkya (content)

An interactive self-assessment. The prospect reads 10 affirmative statements about a well-run marketing operation, and slides each on a **0–10 scale** based on how true it is for *his* company. His own low scores reveal the brutal gaps — then he receives an honest diagnosis and a reason to talk to us.

---

## 1. THE PSYCHOLOGY (why it's built this way — don't change this)

- **Affirmative framing = self-realisation.** Every statement describes the *ideal*. When he rates it low, *he* is admitting the gap — nobody is accusing him. Self-admitted truth converts far better than being told.
- **The slider makes him think.** Dragging a slider forces a considered answer, not a lazy yes/no. The small effort creates ownership of the result.
- **Honesty builds trust (critical).** The tool must be genuinely diagnostic — a high scorer is told the truth: *"you're ahead — you may not need us yet."* A tool honest enough to say that is a tool worth trusting. **Do NOT rig every path to "book a call."** (See build notes.)

---

## 2. HOW IT WORKS ON THE PAGE

1. Prospect sees the section: *"Where does your marketing really stand? Be honest — this is for you."*
2. 10 statements, each with a **0–10 slider** (0 = "Not at all true" · 10 = "Completely true").
3. Grouped in 3 blocks: **Strategic Clarity → Execution → Results** (in that order — it mirrors how growth actually works: clarity drives execution drives results).
4. On submit → an honest score, a category breakdown, a diagnosis archetype, and one clear next step.
5. Lead capture (name, company, phone) to reveal the *full* result / send it to him.

---

## 3. THE QUESTIONS

### Block A — Strategic Clarity (the foundation)
*"How clear is your growth thinking?"*

1. Marketing runs as a defined function with clear ownership.
2. I have a written, deliberate go-to-market strategy.
3. I know precisely which customers I want to win.
4. My full factory capability is visible to the market.

### Block B — Execution (clarity turned into action)
*"How consistently do you act on it?"*

5. We market consistently every week, without long silences.
6. A skilled, dedicated person owns our marketing execution.
7. Our sales team enters meetings with capability-proving tools.

### Block C — Results (what the effort returns)
*"What is it actually producing?"*

8. New enquiries arrive predictably, month after month.
9. Buyers trust our credibility before the first call.
10. Our marketing spend clearly drives measurable growth.

---

## 4. THE "BRUTAL FACT" BEHIND EACH LOW SCORE

*(Use these as result-page insights, or hover tooltips. This is where thought leadership shows — each names the real cost of a low score.)*

1. **No owner → no growth.** A function nobody owns is a function that never improves.
2. **No GTM → you talk to everyone and reach no one.** Effort scatters without a strategy.
3. **Unclear target → wasted effort on wrong buyers**, while the right ones go to competitors.
4. **Hidden capability → you compete on price**, not on the 20–30 years of skill you built.
5. **Stop-start effort resets every time** — like heating metal and never forging it.
6. **Untrained part-time hand → your market face looks unserious** to serious buyers.
7. **No sales tools → your team can't prove capability** in the first 5 minutes that decide the deal.
8. **No predictable enquiries → growth is luck**, and one slow customer becomes a crisis.
9. **No pre-trust → every meeting starts from zero**, and buyers pick whoever looks credible.
10. **Spend not linked to growth → marketing stays an "expense"**, never an investment.

---

## 5. SCORING MODEL

**Total score: out of 100** (10 questions × 10).
Also calculate **3 category scores** so the diagnosis is specific:
- Strategic Clarity: out of 40 (Q1–4)
- Execution: out of 30 (Q5–7)
- Results: out of 30 (Q8–10)

### Overall archetypes (honest, brutal-but-fair):

**0–40 — "The Invisible Factory"**
> A genuinely good factory the market cannot see. Right now, growth depends on old references and luck. The gap is real — but it is also the fastest one to fix, because you are starting with a clean slate and a strong product. This is exactly the stage where structure changes everything.
> *Next step: this is worth a serious conversation. Book a GTM Readiness Conversation.*

**41–70 — "The Inconsistent Effort"**
> You have started — there is some clarity or some activity. But it is not consistent, and it is not compounding, so results stay unpredictable. You are leaving your best growth on the table not from lack of trying, but from lack of structure. Small fixes here often unlock large jumps.
> *Next step: let's find where the leak is. Book a GTM Readiness Conversation.*

**71–100 — "The Structured Grower" (honest high-score path)**
> You are ahead of most manufacturers — real clarity, real consistency, real results. Honestly, you may not need us yet. But even strong operators usually have one weak link (often credibility before the meeting, or linking spend to growth). If you want a sharp outside eye on that one gap, we're happy to talk. If not, take this result as a well-earned green flag.
> *Next step (optional): a focused second-opinion conversation.*

## 6. THE SMART DIAGNOSIS — read the PATTERN, not just the total

*(This is what makes the tool feel expert. Compare the 3 category scores and show the matching insight.)*

- **Clarity HIGH, Execution LOW:** *"You know what to do — you're just not doing it consistently. Your gap is an execution engine, not more thinking."*
- **Execution HIGH, Results LOW:** *"You're working hard on marketing but it's not paying back — a classic sign of effort pointed at the wrong strategy."*
- **Clarity LOW, Results OK:** *"Your results are coming from luck or old relationships, not a system. That is fragile — the day references slow down, so does growth."*
- **All three LOW:** *"You're starting near-zero — which is actually good news. With a clean slate and a strong factory, structured marketing can move you fast."*
- **All three HIGH:** *"You're a rare, well-run growth operation. Protect the consistency — and watch the one category that scored lowest."*

---

## 7. BUILD NOTES FOR THE ENGINEER

- **Sliders default to 5** (neutral) so he must consciously move each — no accidental zeros.
- **Keep it on one screen** if possible; 10 sliders is short. No multi-page friction.
- **Honesty is mandatory:** the 71–100 path must genuinely soft-pedal the sale. Do not force every result to "book a call." This is the trust-builder — protect it.
- **Single CTA** everywhere it appears: *"Book a GTM Readiness Conversation."*
- **Lead capture:** show the headline score instantly; ask for name/company/phone to reveal the full category breakdown + diagnosis (or WhatsApp/email it to him). This turns the tool into a lead source.
- **Mobile-first:** many promoters open on phone via WhatsApp — sliders must work cleanly on touch.
- **No fake urgency** in any result message. The honest archetypes above are the whole persuasion.
